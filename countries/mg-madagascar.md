| Language | Status | Also spoken in SA? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Malagasy** | **Official** — **an Austronesian language** | No | [1] |
| French | **Official** | No | [1] |

**Official-language instrument:** `[NEEDS SOURCE]`.

**Languages Ubuntu Heritage does not have:** both.

🔴 **Malagasy's closest relatives are in Indonesia, not Africa.** Source [1]: Madagascar "was first
permanently settled during or before the mid-first millennium CE (roughly 500 to 700) by **Austronesian
peoples, presumably arriving on outrigger canoes from present-day Indonesia**" — with the first
settlers arriving **from South Borneo** [1].

**Every other language in this folder is Afroasiatic, Niger-Congo, Nilo-Saharan, Khoisan, Indo-European
or a creole of those.** Malagasy is none of them. **It is the westernmost Austronesian language on
Earth**, spoken by an African country's whole population.

**Why this matters to Ubuntu Heritage specifically.** The app's language registry is built around
eleven South African languages, most of them Bantu. This folder has been steadily extending outward
through related and unrelated African families. **Madagascar is the point at which "African language"
stops being a coherent technical category and becomes a political and geographical one** — and that is
worth the app saying out loud rather than quietly mis-shelving.

**Same structural blocker as Burkina Faso** — see [`bf-burkina-faso.md`](bf-burkina-faso.md#languages).

## National anthem

- **Title (own language):** ***Ry Tanindrazanay malala ô*** (Malagasy)
- **Title (English):** "Oh, Our Beloved Fatherland"
- **Adopted:** **27 April 1959** — **before independence**
- **Words by:** Pasteur Rahajason
- **Music by:** Norbert Raharisoa
- **Recording we could use:** none sourced yet
- **Source:** [2]

> **Adopted by parliament fourteen months before independence** [2], while Madagascar was an
> autonomous republic within the French Union — so the country had its own official song before it
> had its own sovereignty. Compare Cameroon, whose anthem became official in 1957 and independent in
> 1960 ([`cm-cameroon.md`](cm-cameroon.md)), and Liberia, whose words date to 1847
> ([`lr-liberia.md`](lr-liberia.md)).
>
> **The title is in Malagasy**, not French — one of the minority in this folder where the country's
> own language leads.

**Framing notes.**

- 🔴 **Madagascar is where two migrations met.** Austronesians from Borneo by 500–700 CE, **Bantu
  speakers from southeastern Africa around 1000 CE** [1]. **The second of those is the same migration
  the app's Atlas material already covers** — the Bantu expansion that reaches Gabon by the 13th
  century BCE ([`ga-gabon.md`](ga-gabon.md)) and Southern Africa later. **Here it crossed the
  Mozambique Channel.** The Journey's story has an island chapter, and the app does not know it yet.
- 🔴 **Queen Ranavalona I ruled for thirty-three years and banned Christianity** [1]. **An African
  queen who expelled a European religion from her kingdom and reigned for a third of a century** is a
  story this project should want — and one usually told in European sources as tyranny
  `[NEEDS SOURCE]`. **The framing needs care and a Malagasy source**, because the received European
  account of her is exactly the kind of thing [AGENTS.md §4](../AGENTS.md) exists to guard against.
  She belongs beside Mantatisi in [`bw-botswana.md`](bw-botswana.md) and Yennenga in
  [`bf-burkina-faso.md`](bf-burkina-faso.md) — **the folder now has three women who led African
  states or armies**, and all three are recorded thinly.
- ⚠️ **The 2025 coup is very recent** [1]. Nothing present-tense about Madagascar's government without
  re-checking.
- ***Tompontany* — "masters of the soil/land"** [1] is a Malagasy term for first-settler status, and it
  is the kind of concept-word this project should carry in the original, as it does with *kgotla* and
  *badimo*.

| Group | Share |

- **The Merina built the kingdom that unified much of the island** [1], and are the largest group at
  26%.
- ⚠️ **Malagasy identity is Austronesian *and* African**, not one or the other [1]. **Do not resolve
  that into a single ancestry story**, and do not reach for admixture percentages — the same rule
  STATUS.md already applied to the genetic-admixture table for South Africa.
- **Famadihana**, the reburial ceremony, and the *hiragasy* performance tradition `[NEEDS SOURCE]` —
  neither is in source [1]. **Malagasy ancestor practice is directly comparable to *badimo* in
  [`bw-botswana.md`](bw-botswana.md)** and would be a strong entry, but it must be sourced and handled
  as living practice, not spectacle.
