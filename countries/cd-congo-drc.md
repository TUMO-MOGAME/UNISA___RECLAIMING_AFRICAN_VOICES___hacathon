# Congo (DRC)

**ISO code:** `cd` · **Region:** Middle (Central) Africa (UN M49)
**Researched by:** Tumo Olorato Mogame, with Claude · **Last updated:** 2026-08-30
**Status:** core research — languages, anthem, milestones and heritage sourced · peoples and literature empty

> ⚠️ **Two different countries are called Congo.** This file is the **Democratic Republic of the
> Congo** (capital **Kinshasa**, ISO `cd`). Its neighbour is the **Republic of the Congo** (capital
> **Brazzaville**, ISO `cg`) — see [`cg-congo-republic.md`](cg-congo-republic.md).

> **How this file is put together.** Built **claim by claim**: every row names the source it came
> from, and the numbered list at the bottom is what those names point at. Sources are
> encyclopaedia-grade — rough sources are fine at this stage ([README.md](README.md)), assertions made
> to a reader are not.
>
> ⚠️ **This file touches one of the largest atrocities in colonial history and an ongoing conflict.**
> Neither belongs in an app as background colour.

## National anthem

- **Title (own language):** *Debout Congolaise* (French)
- **Title (English):** "Arise, Congolese"
- **Adopted:** **30 June 1960** — independence day · replaced **1971** (as Zaire) · **restored 17 May
  1997**
- **Words by:** **Joseph Lutumba**
- **Music by:** **Simon-Pierre Boka Di Mpasi Londi**
- **Recording we could use:** none sourced yet
- **Source:** [2]

> **Source [2] records something no other anthem in this folder does:** it was **"specifically written
> for specific soloist and choral parts in the lyrics"**, which source [2] suggests may be unique
> among national anthems.
>
> **That is directly interesting to this project.** An anthem scored for a solo voice answering a
> chorus is **call-and-response** — the structural basis of a great deal of African oral performance.
> If the app ever handles audio for this country, that form is the thing to preserve, not flatten into
> a single massed vocal. `[NEEDS SOURCE]` for the call-and-response reading specifically; source [2]
> gives the soloist/choral fact, and the interpretation is ours and should be checked.
>
> **Dropped when the country became Zaire in 1971, restored with the country's name in 1997** [2] —
> the anthem, the name and the flag moved together, twice.

## Milestones

| Year | Milestone | One grounded line | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1879 | Leopold II's International Association of the Congo | | [1] |
| **1885 (1 Jul)** | **The Congo Free State** | Created as **Leopold II's personal possession** — not a Belgian colony, but one man's property. | [1] |
| **1908 (15 Nov)** | **The Belgian Congo** | Leopold cedes the territory after international pressure over **"widespread atrocities"**, following the **Casement Report**. | [1] |
| **1960 (30 Jun)** | **Independence from Belgium** | **Patrice Lumumba** becomes first Prime Minister. | [1] |
| 1960 (20 Sep) | Admitted to the UN | | [1] |
| **1961 (17 Jan)** | **Lumumba executed** | By **"Belgian-led Katangan troops"** — source [1]'s wording. | [1] |
| 1965 | Mobutu's coup | The country is declared a "Democratic Republic". | [1] |
| **1971** | **Renamed Zaire** | The national symbols, including the anthem, are changed. | [1], [2] |
| **1996–1997** | **The First Congo War** | Mobutu's rule ends, 17 May 1997; the name and anthem are restored. | [1], [2] |
| **1998–2003** | **The Second Congo War** | President Laurent-Désiré Kabila is assassinated. | [1] |
| 2018 | Tshisekedi wins a contested election | Source [1] calls it the **first peaceful transition of power since independence**. | [1] |

**Framing notes.**

- 🔴 **The Congo Free State was a private possession, and what happened in it is among the largest
  atrocities in colonial history.** Source [1] gives "widespread atrocities" and the **Casement
  Report** [1] — the document that exposed it. **Ubuntu Heritage must not use a euphemism here**, and
  equally must not reach for a death toll: the figures are contested among historians and putting an
  unsourced number in an app would be its own failure. **State that it happened, name the Casement
  Report, and cite real scholarship.** This is the most serious `[NEEDS SOURCE]` in the folder.
- 🔴 **Lumumba was the first Prime Minister and was executed within seven months** [1] — by
  "Belgian-led Katangan troops", per source [1]'s own phrasing. He is one of the defining figures of
  African independence, and the manner of his death is central to how the continent's post-colonial
  history is understood. Treat with the same care as Cabral's assassination
  ([`gw-guinea-bissau.md`](gw-guinea-bissau.md)) and Sankara's
  ([`bf-burkina-faso.md`](bf-burkina-faso.md)). **This folder now records three liberation leaders
  killed: 1961, 1973, 1987.** That is a pattern, and it should be told as one.
- ⚠️ **"First peaceful transition of power since independence" in 2018** [1] — fifty-eight years. State
  it with the date, not as a general characterisation of the country.
- **Renaming: Zaire 1971 → DRC 1997** [1]. With Dahomey→Benin ([`bj-benin.md`](bj-benin.md)) and Upper
  Volta→Burkina Faso ([`bf-burkina-faso.md`](bf-burkina-faso.md)), this folder now has **three
  countries that changed their own names**, each for different reasons and with different outcomes.

## Peoples & cultures

  inscription stand in for the people who live there.

## Heritage & sites

**Five UNESCO World Heritage Sites — all natural, and four of them in danger** [3]:

| Site | Inscribed | Type | In danger | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Virunga National Park** | 1979 | Natural | **Yes** | Active volcanoes; **mountain gorillas** |
| **Garamba National Park** | 1980 | Natural | **Yes** | Forest-to-savanna transition; historically **northern white rhinos** |
| **Kahuzi-Biéga National Park** | 1980 | Natural | **Yes** | East African Rift forest; **eastern lowland gorillas** |
| **Salonga National Park** | 1984 | Natural | No — **danger-listed 1999–2021** | Largely undisturbed rainforest; **bonobos** and forest elephants |
| **Okapi Wildlife Reserve** | 1997 | Natural | **Yes** | Ituri Rainforest refugium; **okapi** and 17 primate species |

Source [3] states the endangered status is due to **poaching, deforestation and armed conflict**.

- ⚠️ **All five sites are natural, and none is cultural.** For a country of 200-plus language
  communities and centuries of kingdoms, **that is a statement about the World Heritage system, not
  about the DRC.** Compare Nigeria's two sites for 525 languages
  ([`ng-nigeria.md`](ng-nigeria.md)). **Ubuntu Heritage should notice that the international heritage
  record catalogues Africa's animals far more readily than its cultures**, and say so.
- **Salonga came off the danger list in 2021** [3] — the sixth recovery recorded in this folder.
